2. **Transparent Attribution:** Where `TRENDVERSE Editorial Team` acts as publisher, clearly retain founder/developer credit for `Vicky` in:
   - The About Us page (`/p/about.html` & in-app `#policy/about`)
   - The Editorial Policy page (`/p/editorial-policy.html` & `#policy/editorial-policy`)
   - The global website footer (`Founded and developed by Vicky · Published by TRENDVERSE Editorial Team`)

If Vicky chooses to add an official headshot later, upload it via Blogger Post/Page Media or GitHub assets and replace the monogram `div` with an explicit `<img>` carrying `width`, `height`, and descriptive `alt="Vicky, Founder and Developer of TRENDVERSE"`.
